Common questions

Which capture formats can I open?

Netvis reads .pcap and .pcapng files. It focuses on Ethernet IPv4 traffic with TCP and UDP parsing, plus selected application protocol details.

Can I use Netvis alongside Wireshark?

Yes. Open the saved capture in Netvis to see the topology, replay traffic, and annotate the moments you want to explain.

Does Netvis capture live traffic?

Netvis analyzes saved captures. You can open files from your existing capture tools without installing a packet capture extension.
