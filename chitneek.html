Questions

A few things to know.

Is Chitneek free?

Yes. The free plan lets you keep up to three books or documents at a time and try the core reading and listening experience. Full access is available through monthly, yearly, or lifetime options.

Can I listen without an internet connection?

Yes. Downloaded audio and supported on-device voices can work entirely offline, so you can keep listening without sending the text to an online service.

Which file types can I add?

Chitneek supports common reading and listening formats including PDF, EPUB, MP3, and MP4. You can also work with scanned pages, recordings, and supported video sources.

Which languages are available for offline voices?

High-quality offline voices are available for Polish, English, German, and French. The exact voices available can depend on your device and language settings.

Can Chitneek read a physical page?

Yes. Use the built-in scanner to capture a page, recognize its text, and add it to your library for reading or listening.

What are shared groups?

Shared groups let people organize and access a collection together. They are useful for families, study groups, or anyone collecting material around the same topic.

Is Chitneek available on iPad?

Chitneek is currently designed primarily for iPhone. It may run on compatible iPads, but the experience is not yet specifically designed for the larger screen.
