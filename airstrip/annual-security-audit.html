Annual Production Security Audit

Access, keys, storage settings, and certificates drift over time. Pull them back to baseline in one sitting.

Bring the perimeter back to baseline

  1. Audit IAM Role PrivilegesReview access and remove unnecessary permissions.
  2. Revoke Stale SSH KeysRemove credentials held by former devices, people, or vendors.
  3. Verify S3 & Database Public Access BlocksConfirm storage and databases cannot be exposed by mistake.
  4. Inspect TLS Certificate Expiration DatesRecord renewal dates and fix certificates close to expiry.
