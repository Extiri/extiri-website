Incident Response & Outage Mitigation

Mitigation comes before understanding. Stabilize first, analyze in the post-mortem, and send an early update.

7 required stepsStrict sequenceIncident response

The first response

  1. Acknowledge the PagerDuty alertTake ownership immediately.
  2. Join the incident channelMove coordination into one visible place.
  3. Update status page to “Investigating”Let affected people know.
  4. Identify failing component via DatadogUse metrics and traces to narrow the impact.
  5. Mitigate impactRollback or scale up, as needed.
  6. Update status page to “Resolved”Close the public loop after verification.
  7. Schedule post-mortem reviewCapture learning after users are safe again.
