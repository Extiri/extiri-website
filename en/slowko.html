Good to know.

The practical details before you download the app.

Does Słówko. need an internet connection?

A connection is needed to fetch the daily word and update widgets. Pronunciation uses voices on your device and works offline.

How do I save a word?

Tap the star beside a word to add it to your favourites. You can browse and search the collection whenever you want to return to it.

How do flashcards, quizzes and crosswords work?

Exercises use words you have encountered in the app. Flashcards let you recall a meaning for yourself, while quizzes and crosswords test recognition and spelling.

Does the widget update automatically?

The widget displays the daily word and refreshes after you open the app. Add it from the widget gallery on your home screen.

What purchase options are available?

Choose a subscription with a 7-day free trial or a one-time purchase for full access. Current regional prices are shown in the App Store and Google Play.
