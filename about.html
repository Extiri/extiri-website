Independent software studio

One person behind every Extiri product.

I’m Wiktor Wójcik. I design, build and ship apps for Mac, iPhone, Android and the web, from the first rough idea to the details people use every day.

Why Extiri exists.

I started Extiri to make software that solves a real problem without creating ten new ones.

Each app begins with something that could be easier: keeping useful code close, understanding a complicated cloud setup, listening to a document, or remembering a new word. I work through the product, interface and engineering together so the result feels coherent rather than assembled.

Staying independent lets me make careful decisions, keep conversations direct and give the less visible parts of a product the same attention as its first screen. Privacy, accessibility and long-term maintainability are part of that work from the beginning.

The work is the introduction.

Extiri spans developer tools, infrastructure software, accessibility and everyday learning. Different audiences, built with the same care for clarity and useful details.

How I approach the work.

Start with the person using it

The interface should make sense before anyone needs an explanation. Features earn their place by helping with a specific task.

Keep privacy practical

I prefer local processing and collect only what a product genuinely needs. Privacy should be reflected in how the app works, not confined to a policy page.

Build for continued use

Clear code, native platform conventions and thoughtful edge cases make an app easier to trust today and maintain later.

Work directly

There is no handoff between a salesperson, designer and developer. The person you speak with is the person making the product.
